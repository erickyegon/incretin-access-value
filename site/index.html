What this shows

  • Coverage raised prescriptions from 1.3 in the first quarter to 17.2 per 1,000 enrollees after eight quarters, and the result holds across estimators.
  • About 17.8 million adults with Medicaid meet the label criteria; 30.4 million U.S. adults currently use a GLP-1 drug.
  • Primary care physicians wrote 55.8% of Part D incretin claims in 2024, which reflect diabetes and other covered uses, not obesity use.
  • The biggest unknown in the budget is the rebate: the central case uses 51.2%, the midpoint of 23.1% (statutory minimum) and 79.3% (implied by the announced $245 price).
